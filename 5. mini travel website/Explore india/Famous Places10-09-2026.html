Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is a stunning white marble mausoleum. Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan built it for his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a global symbol of love.

Red Fort

The Red Fort is a historic fort made of red sandstone. It served as the main residence of the Mughal emperors. Today, it is an important national symbol where the Prime Minister hoists the flag on Independence Day.

Qutub Minar

The Qutub Minar is the world's tallest brick minaret, standing at 72.5 meters. Built in the 12th century, this red sandstone tower is famous for its intricate carvings and early Indo-Islamic architecture.

Golden Temple

The Golden Temple, also known as Sri Harmandir Sahib, is the holiest shrine in Sikhism. It features a striking gold-covered dome resting on a marble base and sits inside a sacred pool of water

Hawa Mahal

Hawa Mahal, or the "Palace of Winds," is a unique five-story palace built from pink and red sandstone. It features 953 small windows called jharokhas designed to let cool air flow through the building.
